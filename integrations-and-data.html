Integrations and data

Give your agents the information and tools they need during a conversation, then review the results in the dashboard.

What the agent can do through MCP

Customer records
Read and update CRM records, including the outcome of an outbound call.
Booking and scheduling
Check available options, offer alternatives, and book, reschedule, or cancel.
Business systems
Connect the relevant ERP, hospital information, or laboratory systems through your APIs.
Messaging tools
Send an SMS or WhatsApp message to confirm an outcome or follow up.

The deck describes the current demonstration as connected to mock systems. Connecting production workflows requires your APIs and agreed rules for what the agent may do.

What your team can review

The admin dashboard shows call volumes, outcomes, and handoffs. Inbound and outbound calls have recordings, live transcripts, and AI summaries. Existing IVR recordings can also feed the quality review and coaching workflow.

Your brand and data

RoboRingo supports white-labelled conversations and customer-approved guardrails. The product deck states that data is hosted in AWS Mumbai and customer data is never used to train AI models.
