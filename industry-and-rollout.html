Getting started

Bring your call-handling SOPs, current call flow, API list, SIP trunk and WhatsApp details, and representative recordings for each scenario.

  1. Connect your systems and agree on flows and guardrails.
  2. Launch inbound calls and messages with handoff to your team.
  3. Enable outbound calls, confirmations, and follow-ups.
  4. Keep improving through training and approved security exercises.
