A few things to know.

Can I keep my existing numbers and IVR?

Yes. Bring your business number and use existing IVR recordings for analysis.

Which channels and languages are supported?

Phone and WhatsApp calls, plus SMS and WhatsApp messages. The suite supports Hindi, English, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam.

Can the AI hand a caller to my team?

Yes. Route to a specialist AI or a person. Ring available agents in round robin, serial, or parallel order, or bring several agents into a group call.

How can I try it?

Call +91 80 6559 0741, choose a language, and ask to speak to a person. You can also book a connected product demo.
