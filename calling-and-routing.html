Calling and routing

See how inbound and outbound conversations reach the right agent and update your systems.

Calls, messages, and actions

Inbound across four channels

One AI agent answers phone and WhatsApp calls around the clock and replies to SMS and WhatsApp messages. It uses approved knowledge, recommends options or alternatives, books, reschedules or cancels appointments, and hands off when needed.

Outbound calls triggered by events

A rule in your system triggers a call in the customer’s language. RoboRingo writes the result back and confirms it over SMS or WhatsApp. Use it for reminders, schedule changes with alternative slots, preparation and document checks, dropped-call callbacks, feedback, renewals, and follow-ups.

Connected tools through MCP

RoboRingo’s MCP server reads and updates CRM, booking, ERP, hospital information, and lab systems. The agent can send SMS or WhatsApp messages and change bookings. The product demonstration uses mock systems. Production workflows require your APIs.

Handoffs and the agent workspace

Send a conversation to a specialist AI or a person. Use round robin, serial priority, parallel ringing, or a group call. When nobody is available, RoboRingo takes a voice note and produces a transcript and summary.

Human agents use a browser softphone to take and transfer calls, set availability, and dial out. Each call has its own web room. Agents join on transfer while the AI stays to monitor, and a dropped caller can be called back into the room.

The dashboard brings together inbound and outbound recordings, live transcripts, summaries, volumes, outcomes, and handoffs. Existing IVR recordings can feed the same review workflow.

How an outbound call works

  1. A rule in your system detects an event, such as an upcoming appointment or a schedule change.
  2. RoboRingo calls in the customer’s language and offers a suitable option or alternative.
  3. The agent saves the outcome in your system through the connected API.
  4. The agent confirms by SMS or WhatsApp and follows up if needed.

How the call room works

Enter the call room

  1. Customer joins by phone
  2. RoboRingo AI answers
  3. Call room opens

The AI handles the conversation using the approved knowledge and rules for that workflow.

Connect business tools

  1. AI receives the request
  2. MCP calls your API
  3. Result returns to the room

The agent can look up CRM records or booking options, take an allowed action, and confirm it through SMS or WhatsApp.

Hand off in the same room

  1. Caller needs a handoff
  2. Routing selects a handler
  3. New handler joins

When a human agent joins, RoboRingo can stay to monitor. If nobody is available, the AI can take a voice note and produce a transcript and summary.

Recover and review

  1. Caller disconnects
  2. AI calls back into the room
  3. Team reviews the record

The dashboard brings together recordings, transcripts, summaries, outcomes, and handoffs.
