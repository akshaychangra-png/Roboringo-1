Training, certification, and readiness

Learn from real calls

  1. Ingest inbound and outbound recordings, including recordings from your existing IVR.
  2. Review SOP adherence, empathy, fluency, grammar, and customer service.
  3. Flag deviations and assign a voice trainer to the agent.
  4. Choose a scenario and practise the skills that need attention.
  5. Certify improvement with a simulated call and repeat the cycle as new calls arrive.

Test readiness before an incident

Approved simulations test whether help-desk or inbound agents follow procedures under pressure. Scenarios are agreed in advance and run with written approval. Responses are scored on verification, refusal, and escalation, then flagged gaps feed into training.

Two ways to find a gap

Review completed calls
Spot missed SOP steps, gaps in empathy, or signs of frustration. Assign a voice practice scenario and check the outcome in a certification call.
Run an approved exercise
Test whether help-desk agents protect information and whether inbound agents follow procedure when a simulated customer pressures them. Feed the findings into the same coaching loop.
